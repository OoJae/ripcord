## Video direction

**The one loud moment is Frame 6 (THE PULL).** Everything before it is deliberately
restrained so it can land, and nothing after it competes. If a frame is unsure of
itself, it should get quieter, not busier.

- **Ground:** `cream` (#0A0B10, the brand void) full-bleed on every frame, as its own
  full-duration `class="clip"` background layer — never on `#root`. Over it, the
  ripstop weave: a 12px hairline grid at ~1.5% ink plus a 96px reinforcement grid at
  ~2.5%. It is the brand's texture (the fabric engineered to arrest a tear) and it is
  the only decoration allowed anywhere in the film.
- **Type doctrine, inherited from the product:** if it is a MEASUREMENT — a health
  factor, a hash, a duration, a rule id, a dollar figure — it is Space Mono. Statements
  are Clash Display, uppercase, tight negative tracking. Never mix the two roles.
- **`phosphor` (#7CF5C4) is scoped.** It may appear ONLY on a value that has been
  recovered or verified: the post-save health factor, an APPROVE, a passing check. It
  is never decoration and never a general accent. Frames 1–5 must contain no phosphor
  at all — that is what makes its first appearance in Frame 6 mean something.
- **`fire-orange` (#FF4F00) is the pull.** Reserve it for danger and for the act of
  pulling. Frames 6 and 13 may flood with it; everywhere else it is a hairline, a
  label, or a single word.
- **Held beats, placed deliberately for rhythm:** Frames 3, 8 and 13 are short and
  still on purpose — the film breathes there. Do not add motion to them.
- **Motion budget:** transform and opacity only. One gesture per scene window. Reveals
  are cued to the spoken line and spread across the back half; never front-load.
- **Footage handling:** the site clips are the real product, captured live. Play them
  full-bleed or in a hairline-bordered plate; never add a fake browser chrome, never
  add a drop shadow, and never scale past 1.15 (they are 1080p natives with no
  headroom).
